# 0009 — Place Stack after What I do

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-21

## Context

The homepage needs to connect the services to the skills that support them before moving into experience and project evidence.

## Decision

Order the homepage sections as Hero, What I do, Stack, Experience, Work, and Contact. Keep the marquee as a supporting band between Work and Contact.

## Consequences

- The Stack follows What I do and appears before Experience.
- The navigation and scroll tracking use the same section order.
