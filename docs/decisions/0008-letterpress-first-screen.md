# 0008 — The first screen is a letterpress sheet

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-21

## Context

The first screen needed to make the portfolio's identity and next actions clear while giving the opening its own visual idea.

## Decision

Present the hero as one letterpress sheet. A pointer-driven light moves across the sheet. On page entry, a short press-close motion settles the sheet into place; as visitors scroll past the hero, it tilts and recedes. The name, role, summary, and links remain complete without motion.

## Consequences

- The hero reads as one printed object rather than a collection of separate illustrations.
- The sheet motion responds to pointer and scroll position, while the content stays available on touch and reduced-motion paths.
